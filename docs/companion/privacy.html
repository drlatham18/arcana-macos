Your journal stays with you.

September 19, 2026 · Pocket preview

Local reviews and reflections

Arcana Pocket reads only files you choose to import. It saves selected reviews and your reflections in this browser’s local storage. The import does not upload their contents to a server. This browser storage is not a separate encrypted vault; someone with access to your browser profile may be able to read it.

Website requests

The site host receives ordinary requests to load application files, which may include your IP address and browser information. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that hosting. No analytics, advertising scripts, or customer tracking are included in Pocket.

Copies, deletion, and recovery

Use Export backup to keep a private copy. Delete review removes that review and its reflection from this browser. Clearing site data removes the journal and offline application cache. It does not remove files in your Files app, desktop exports, backups, or copies you shared. Browser storage can be evicted by the device; keep a backup of anything important.

Desktop licensing

Windows paid access is not yet available. The planned payment provider is Stripe; desktop licensing and its final privacy disclosures are still being verified. Pocket itself does not collect payment information or license keys. It is a review reader, not a recorder or a cloud synchronization service.

Support

Contact supportarcana@gmail.com. Latham Counseling LLC operates Arcana. Support messages are handled through Gmail; send only what is needed to explain the problem. Do not send passwords, payment details, or license keys. See the business privacy notice.
