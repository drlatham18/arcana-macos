What we’re building

Desktop first. Your notes stay yours.

Windows desktop software

A downloadable application for Windows x64 PCs. Match recording and saved reviews are designed to run alongside your separately installed Arena game.

Local analysis and study

A rule-based engine provides limited study prompts and search. The current engine does not use a paid AI API, and there are no AI credit packages to buy.

An iPhone supplement

A browser-based review journal is in development. It reads selected exports from the desktop app; it cannot record Arena games played on iOS and is not a native App Store release.

Required: turn on Detailed Logs

In MTG Arena, open Settings → View Account → Detailed Logs (Plugin Support), then restart Arena. Arcana needs these logs to record and review your matches.

Analysis is limited by the available logs and supported interactions. It cannot guarantee the best play or improved results. Arcana is an independent study aid, not an official Wizards product. Live assistance must not be used in competitive, tournament, or prize-bearing matches.
