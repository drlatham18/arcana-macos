Limits that matter

  • The recorder needs Detailed Logs, readable files, and a running, unpaused Arcana engine.
  • Incomplete or changed Arena logs can limit or break reviews.
  • The engine uses limited heuristics and visible information. It cannot calculate a guaranteed best move or evaluate every card interaction.
  • Live assistance must not be used in competitive, ranked, tournament, or prize-bearing matches.
