✧ ARCANA

SEPTEMBER 16, 2026 · VERSION 1.3

Your study stays on your Mac.

Arcana does not require an account, an API key, or a cloud subscription. The desktop engine runs locally. It does not upload your Arena logs, match history, rules files, or notes.

What the app reads and saves

With your configuration, Arcana reads Arena’s local game log and card database. It stores preferences, an index of study materials, notes, and completed-game reviews in your Mac’s Application Support/Arcana folder. The card database is opened read-only. An export creates the selected review file only when you choose to export it.

External websites

Links to official rules, support, and downloads open your browser. This public site and its downloads are hosted by GitHub, whose infrastructure may process IP addresses and request logs under GitHub’s privacy statement. We have added no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies to this site. GitHub provides aggregate repository and download counts; those counts do not establish unique or active users.

Sharing and feedback

GitHub issues are public. Do not post raw Arena logs, account identifiers, private notes, or personal information. Share a minimal description of a bug and the steps needed to reproduce it. You choose whether to share a review or submit an issue.

Removing local data

Quit Arcana before removing its Application Support/Arcana folder to remove stored notes, settings, imported-rule indexes, and review history. This does not remove your original Arena logs or any review files you exported elsewhere.
